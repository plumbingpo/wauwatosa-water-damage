Roof Leak Water Damage Help in Wauwatosa

A roof leak rarely starts where the stain appears. A loose shingle, a failed flashing joint or an aging seal around a skylight lets rain in, and it travels across the roof deck and framing until it falls into the insulation. By the time a brown ring shows on the ceiling, the attic above it has often been wet for a while. We find the full wet area and dry it out.

Our trade is water damage restoration, and roofing is a separate one. We work on the interior, which means removing wet insulation, drying drywall and framing, dealing with mold and rebuilding the ceiling. If rain is still coming in, we can tarp the roof as a temporary cover, or work alongside the roofer you choose so the leak is fixed before we close anything up.

Do This First

Your First Steps

  1. Shut Off Wet CircuitsTurn off the breaker for any light, fan or outlet that has water running through or near it, then keep your hands off it.
  2. Contain the WaterFurniture, electronics and rugs should be moved aside, with buckets placed wherever water is falling. Lay towels or plastic sheeting down to protect the floor.
  3. Drain a Bulging CeilingIf the ceiling is holding a pocket of water, stand to one side and poke a small hole at the lowest point so it drains into a bucket.
  4. Stay Off the RoofA wet roof and an unlit attic are both places to stay out of. Note from inside where the water is coming in and leave the rest to professionals.
  5. Photograph the DamageUse your phone to record the damaged ceiling, attic and belongings first, hold off on throwing things out until that is done, and call us to get the drying started.
Common Causes

What Causes It

  • Popped or exposed roofing nails, each one a tiny opening for rain.
  • Corroded or loose metal flashing where the roof meets a chimney, a vent or a wall.
  • A valley liner that has worn through at the point where the most runoff passes.
  • Sun-cracked rubber collars where plumbing vent pipes come through the roof.
  • Storm impact, such as hail or a tree branch, breaking through the shingles.
If It Is Left

What Happens if You Wait

Insulation in the Attic

For days after a leak, matted insulation sits wet on top of the ceiling and no longer insulates. Drying is sometimes possible with fiberglass. Cellulose that is saturated generally comes out.

Inside the Walls

Rain sometimes follows the framing into a wall, where the insulation and the hidden side of the drywall take it up. Often the one clue is paint that blisters or trim that swells at the floor.

Drywall Ceilings

Drywall that stays wet stains, sags and loses its strength. The tape releases along cracked seams, and with enough weight a section can come down suddenly.

Ceiling Fixtures and Wiring

Recessed cans, ceiling fans and electrical boxes are low points where roof water collects and drips through. Keep that circuit off until someone has inspected it, since damp wiring may corrode or short out.

Insurance

What About Insurance?

Interior damage from a sudden, accidental roof leak, like one caused by a fallen limb or wind-torn shingles, is often covered under a homeowners policy. A leak put down to an old, worn or neglected roof is usually denied, and fixing the roof may be treated separately from the interior work. We document the damage with photos and moisture readings for your adjuster, but coverage depends on your policy, so read it or ask your agent.

What It Costs

What Affects the Cost

  • Drywall removal: how much of the ceiling and walls is beyond saving.
  • How far down the leak traveled, from the attic to the walls and floor.
  • Attic access, ceiling height and whether a temporary tarp is needed first.
  • How much repainting is required so the patched ceiling blends in.

What We Do

How We Handle Roof Leak Water Damage

1

Trace the Leak Path

Moisture meters and a thermal camera let us follow the rain through the attic, the ceilings and the walls, so spots that look dry from underneath are not missed.

2

Cover and Protect

When rain can still get in, a temporary tarp from us or a call to your roofer comes first. Floors and belongings below the wet ceiling are covered as well.

3

Remove and Dry

After removing the insulation and drywall that are past saving, our crew dries the attic and lower rooms with air movers and dehumidifiers and tracks moisture levels until the wood is dry.

4

Treat and Rebuild

Where it is called for, we apply an antimicrobial to the bare framing. With the roof fixed, our team installs insulation and drywall, finishes the joints, and primes and paints so the repair blends in.

Few water damage events are as common, or as potentially devastating to a property, as a pipe that bursts. A single burst pipe can release 4 to 8 gallons of water per minute, which is 240 to 480 gallons per hour flooding through your walls, ceilings and floors. The trigger may be a hard freeze, corroded or aging lines, high pressure or physical damage, but the need is the same: act immediately so water does less harm to the structure, the floors, your possessions and the building's systems.

Day or night, Wauwatosa Restoration responds to burst pipe emergencies across Wauwatosa and the nearby communities. We can help find the shutoff and stop the water, and our team works with licensed plumbers to see that the pipe itself is repaired correctly. As soon as the flow is shut down, extraction starts: portable and truck-mounted units pull standing water out of every affected area as fast as we can.

Structural drying comes next and covers the wet drywall, the framing, the insulation, the subfloor and the finished flooring above it. Since water runs downhill through ceiling spaces, floor assemblies and wall cavities, a burst pipe frequently affects several rooms on more than one level. Our thermal imaging and moisture detection equipment traces water migration paths so every affected area is identified and properly dried, which prevents the mold growth and structural deterioration that hidden moisture causes.

Warning Signs for Burst Pipe Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Faucets that give only a trickle, or nothing, during a freeze
  • Ceilings or walls that show damp patches, stains or a swollen bulge
  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture
  • Exposed pipes that drip, mist or shoot water
  • Rusty or discolored water coming from your taps
  • A musty smell near a wall, cabinet or floor with plumbing behind it
  • An unusually high water bill that points to a hidden leak

Maintenance Tips for Burst Pipe Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Before winter, drain outside spigots and take the hoses off them
  • A plumbing inspection by a licensed pro every few years catches corrosion and wear early
  • Monitor your water pressure, because excessive pressure (above 80 PSI) strains pipes and fittings
  • When it freezes, let taps drip and open the cabinets under sinks to let warm air reach the pipes
  • Aging galvanized or polybutylene plumbing should be swapped out ahead of an unexpected failure
  • Everyone in the house should be able to locate and turn the main water valve in a hurry

Why Professional Burst Pipe Repair Matters

The damage from a burst pipe tends to reach well past the wet areas in plain view. Materials get saturated in spots that can stay undiscovered for days or weeks, because the water runs along conduits, joists, floor assemblies and the insides of walls. By then, mold may already be established in hidden spaces and structural materials may be significantly weakened. With moisture meters and thermal cameras, trained technicians follow the water's path and pinpoint every affected area right away.

Consumer tools cannot compare with the scale of professional drying and extraction equipment. A truck-mounted unit clears water at hundreds of times the speed of a shop vac, while a commercial dehumidifier takes 10 to 20 times more moisture out of the air than a household model. That speed and power are essential when you are racing the 24 to 48 hour window before mold growth begins.
